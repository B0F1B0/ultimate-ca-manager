## How to sign

You sign in your own pull request, with your own commit. There is no bot and nothing
to click.

Add one line to `signatures/cla-v1.1.md`, in the same pull request as your change:

```
| your-github-username | 2026-09-16 | I have read and agree to the UCM CLA v1.1 |
```

A check on the pull request tells you whether every author it contains has signed,
including anyone listed as `Co-authored-by`.

Your signature applies to **every contribution you have made to UCM, past and
future**, including any submitted before this agreement existed. You will not be
asked again.
